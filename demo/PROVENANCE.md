## Risk note

Public-Domain status rests on US copyright law (golden-age comics published
without a valid copyright notice / without renewal) and the source platform's
labelling. The Lev Gleason titles (Crime Does Not Pay, Boy Comics) and the
Columbia title (Sparky Watts) are long-established public-domain golden-age
comics; Prize Comics #2 is labelled Public Domain on Wikimedia Commons. No
trademarked publisher characters (Disney/DC/Marvel) are included. The linked
source is authoritative — verify there in case of doubt.
